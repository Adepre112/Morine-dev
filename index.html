Questions

Everything you’re wondering.

Is Morine really free?

Yes. The free plan covers your AI career profile, CV scoring, job matching and skill-gap insights. Upgrade only when you want premium interview coaching and advanced analytics.

I have little or no experience. Will I be at a disadvantage?

Never. Morine is built for job seekers at every level — whether you're starting out, switching careers or climbing the ladder. We match you against roles that fit your skills and education, weigh your projects and certifications, and help you present them as the experience they truly are.

How does the AI improve my CV?

Our engine reads your CV and the roles you want, then scores it against recruiter and ATS standards — rewording bullets, adding missing keywords and fixing structure in one click.

How long until I get my first interview?

Timing depends on the market you're in, but a complete profile dramatically improves your odds. Morine's match quality rises with every detail you add — so start simple and refine as you go.

Which locations and industries does Morine cover?

Morine covers job seekers across every region of Nigeria — Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Kano, Ibadan, Osogbo, Enugu, Calabar and remote roles — spanning tech, business, healthcare, education, agriculture, creative and engineering. Industries and locations keep growing as the platform scales.
